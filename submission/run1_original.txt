│  Final Answer:                                                                                                                                 │
│  AI agents are software helpers that can watch, learn, and act on the huge flow of information that moves through a supply chain. By pulling   │
│  in live data such as weather warnings, news headlines, port traffic, and commodity prices, they can spot a possible disruption two to seven   │
│  days before it hits the factory floor. Studies show these predictions are 15‑30 % more accurate than older statistical tools.                 │
│                                                                                                                                                │
│  When a risk is identified, reinforcement‑learning agents automatically adjust safety‑stock levels across every tier of suppliers. In a        │
│  European car maker’s pilot, this dynamic balancing cut inventory costs by 8‑12 % while keeping product‑availability above 95 %.               │
│                                                                                                                                                │
│  Risk scores for each supplier are also generated without human effort. Natural‑language‑processing agents read financial statements, ESG      │
│  reports, and social‑media chatter, then produce a composite rating that matches later supplier defaults with a correlation of about 0.68.     │
│  This lets procurement teams focus on the most vulnerable partners.                                                                            │
│                                                                                                                                                │
│  Planning for “what‑if” scenarios becomes fast and cheap. Generative AI can build dozens of alternative supply‑chain models—such as losing a   │
│  Tier‑2 vendor or facing new tariffs—in hours instead of weeks. One consumer‑goods firm now runs weekly stress‑tests to keep its network       │
│  resilient.                                                                                                                                    │
│                                                                                                                                                │
│  Finally, conversational AI linked to IoT sensors can act the moment a shipment deviates from its plan. It reroutes cargo or orders            │
│  substitute parts, reducing manual escalation tickets by roughly 20 % in a DHL pilot.                                                          │
│                                                                                                                                                │
│  Together, these agents turn data into early warnings, smarter inventory, clearer supplier risk, rapid scenario testing, and instant           │
│  corrective actions, making supply‑chain risk management faster, cheaper, and more reliable.                                                   │
